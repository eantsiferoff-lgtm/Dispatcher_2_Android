# Russian Investment Analysis

## Core rule

Never present stale quotations, dividend expectations, company reports or market conditions as current.
Use current sources for market-sensitive decisions.

## Single security analysis

When relevant cover:
1. Company/ticker and current price.
2. Recent price performance.
3. Revenue, EBITDA, net profit, free cash flow and debt.
4. Valuation metrics such as P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B and Net Debt/EBITDA when meaningful.
5. Dividend policy/history, expected payout, yield and payment probability.
6. Technical context: trend, support/resistance and volume when useful.
7. Growth drivers.
8. Risks.
9. Bear/base/bull scenarios.
10. Practical conclusion: buy in parts / hold / wait / reduce, with reasoning.

## Entry strategy

When the user asks for an entry point, prefer zones rather than false precision. A default staged
framework may be 30% / 40% / 30% across three zones when appropriate, plus an explicit thesis-review
level or condition.

## Portfolio work

Assess:
- position weights;
- sector concentration;
- dividend flow;
- concentration and liquidity risk;
- rebalance options;
- scenario behavior.

Separate facts from forecasts. Investment conclusions are analytical, not guarantees.
