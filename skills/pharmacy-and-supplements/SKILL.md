# Pharmacy and Supplements

## Goal

Provide current, practical product/pharmacy information while keeping medical safety boundaries clear.

## Workflow

1. Identify country/city and the product goal.
2. For time-sensitive availability, pricing or local brands, use current sources.
3. Compare the preferred option plus good alternatives.
4. For each option include, when available:
   - active ingredients;
   - strength/dose form;
   - manufacturer;
   - package size;
   - current approximate price;
   - important differences.
5. For supplements, compare meaningful ingredient doses, evidence quality and course cost rather
   than relying on marketing claims.
6. Rank options when the user wants a choice.
7. If requested, prepare one concise message to a pharmacy with preferred and fallback products.

## Safety

- Do not present a supplement as a substitute for necessary medical treatment.
- For prescription medicines or individualized treatment decisions, distinguish general information
  from medical prescribing and recommend appropriate clinician/pharmacist confirmation.
- Flag important interaction/contraindication concerns when materially relevant.
- Do not invent availability or current prices.
